## The gap has flipped direction, which is what success looks like here

Origio used to be missing product the channels had. Now it holds product the
channels don't — which is the expected consequence of importing 1,203 colorways,
and it turns `MISSING_FROM_*` from a backlog into a **publishing queue**.

The two that grew break down cleanly:

| Group | Rows | What it actually is |
|---|---:|---|
| `VN-ONLN-*` missing from Shopify | 422 | **Never listed, not sold.** I assumed sold and checked: **zero** of the 422 appear in Shopify even as archived, and they carry ~308 units in Cin7. This is unlisted sellable vintage, so the answer is publish, not retire. |
| `VPACK25-*` | 40 | Classified `MERCHANDISE`, absent from both channels. Probably not channel product at all — a `CHANNEL_POLICY` case like imperfects and vintage. |
| `B2B-EPL-*` | 23 | Same shape. A wholesale line, not a shop or webshop line. |
| `LIV-XWLWTC-*` | 20 | The Wollow Tarmac collab — real product, genuinely absent from both, and 21 of the 53 blank barcodes. |
