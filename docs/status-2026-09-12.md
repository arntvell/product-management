## Integrity now enforced

- **Barcode uniqueness** — a duplicate is impossible, not merely unlikely.
- **Canonical form and check digits** on every write path.
- **A ledger of issued numbers**, so allocation cannot reissue a printed code.
- **`Colorway.kind`** — merchandise / aggregate / material / consumable / sample
  / service / test, in the model rather than in a script.
- **Provenance** — `FieldOwner` carries the authority, evidence and date behind a
  value, at variant level.
- **Locks** — a disputed value cannot leave the building.

## What went wrong today, and what it cost

**The first full import failed halfway**, writing 1,202 colorways and no
variants. Cause: 11 barcodes are shared by two SKUs each *inside the Cin7 import
set*. I had checked incoming barcodes against Origio, found none, and stopped —
the batch collides with itself, which that check cannot see. The same guard
already existed on the top-up path, written an hour earlier, and was not applied
to the main path. Recovered exactly via a scoped cleanup; re-run clean.

**13 Barnes Japan Dawn products disappeared from Sitoo** during the first live
push. Not reproducible in the sandbox across two attempts, and my client issues
only `PUT {barcode}`, but the cause is still unknown and the products had to be
recreated by hand. Sitoo's audit log on ids 473–485 would settle it.

The lesson in both: the sandbox verified the API's *semantics* and not the
*shape of the run*. `scripts/sitoo/rehearse.py` now does the latter, and the
runbook requires it.
